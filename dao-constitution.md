# Sick Buffalo DAO Constitution

## Preamble

Sick Buffalo DAO exists to create a sustainable, community-driven ecosystem centered on $SICKB and its real-world integrations. This Constitution defines the rights, responsibilities, and governance structure of the DAO.

## Membership

- DAO membership is open to all holders of gBUFFS (governance token).
- Members may propose, discuss, and vote on DAO matters.
